Recipe 1

Slow tomato soup

Serves 4 · 45 min

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, a pinch of sugar

Method

  1. Soften the onion in oil, ten gentle minutes.
  2. Add garlic, then the halved tomatoes.
  3. Cover and cook down for half an hour.
  4. Blend, season, and add the sugar last.

Recipe 2

Skillet flatbread

Makes 6 · 25 min

  • 250 g flour
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp yoghurt
  • A splash of oil

Method

  1. Mix everything into a soft dough and rest it.
  2. Divide into six and roll thin.
  3. Cook in a hot dry pan until puffed and spotted.
  4. Stack under a towel to stay soft.

Do not skip the rest.

Recipe 3

Lemon olive-oil cake

Serves 8 · 55 min

  • 3 eggs
  • 150 g sugar
  • 120 ml olive oil
  • 2 lemons, zest and juice
  • 180 g flour

Method

  1. Whisk eggs and sugar until pale.
  2. Stream in oil, then lemon.
  3. Fold in flour and bake at 170°C.
  4. Cool before slicing. It improves overnight.

Best the next morning.
