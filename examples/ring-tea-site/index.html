Three small rituals

  1. 01

    Warm the pot

    A splash of hot water first, so the leaf wakes up evenly.

  2. 02

    Watch the clock

    Each tea has its own time and temperature. Set a timer and trust it.

  3. 03

    Pour it all

    Leave no water on the leaf, or the second cup turns bitter.
