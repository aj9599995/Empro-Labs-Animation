How a home purchase unfolds, one step at a time.

Scroll to follow the route. Each step is general guidance. The exact order and timing vary by region, so check the local rules.

Start the route

01

Search and shortlist

Set a budget that includes fees and a buffer. Shortlist areas by commute, schools and services, then compare homes on the same criteria.

02

Viewings

Visit at different times of day. Note the light, the noise and the access, and photograph the details you want to check later.

03

Offer

Agree the price and the conditions in writing. Ask what is included in the sale, and what is not.

04

Survey and mortgage

Order a survey and confirm the finance. These checks run in parallel, not one after another, which keeps the timeline short.

05

Completion

Funds are transferred and the keys change hands. Read the final paperwork before you sign it.
