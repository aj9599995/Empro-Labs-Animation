A year in the garden

Twelve months,
one scroll.

What to sow, what to prune, and when to leave it alone. Scroll slowly. The cards stack as the seasons turn.

01 · January

Plan & rest

Order seed, clean your tools, and sketch next year's beds while the ground sleeps.

03 · March

Start indoors

Tomatoes, peppers and herbs go in trays on the sunniest sill you have.

05 · May

Plant out

Frost is gone. Harden the seedlings off for a week, then give them the bed.

07 · July

Water deep

A long soak twice a week beats a daily sprinkle. Mulch to hold it in.

09 · September

Harvest & save

Pick it all, dry the beans, and save seed from your best plants.

11 · November

Tuck it in

Spread compost, cover the beds, and put the hose away. See you in January.

Slow gardens grow best.
